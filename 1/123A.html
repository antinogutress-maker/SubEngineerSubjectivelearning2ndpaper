1. Reference from Datum

The Starting Point: All elevations are measured relative to a fixed reference surface, usually Mean Sea Level (Datum). A Benchmark (BM) is a fixed physical point on the ground whose Reduced Level (R.L.) is accurately known.

2. Establishing L.O.S (H.I.)

Height of Instrument: The level is set up and a Back Sight (B.S.) is taken on the staff held at the Benchmark.
H.I. = Known R.L. + Back Sight (B.S.)

3. Calculation of New R.L.

Finding Unknown Elevations: The staff is moved to a new unknown point. A Fore Sight (F.S.) or Intermediate Sight (I.S.) is taken from the same instrument setup.
New R.L. = H.I. - Fore Sight (F.S.)

4. Arithmetic Check

Verifying Calculations: A mathematical rule used in the field book to ensure no addition/subtraction errors were made during the H.I. method.
Σ B.S. - Σ F.S. = Last R.L. - First R.L.
