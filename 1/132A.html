Plane Tabling Methods

Surveying Visuals

AUTO-LOOP

1. Radiation

One Station Setup: The table is set up at a single station. Rays are drawn from this center to various details (trees, corners). Distances are measured physically and plotted to scale on the paper.
✓ Best for Small, Open Areas where all points are accessible.

2. Intersection

Two Station Setup: The table is set up at station A, a ray is drawn to the target. Then moved to station B (the baseline), and another ray is drawn. The target is plotted exactly where the two rays intersect on paper.
✓ Best for Inaccessible Points (Rivers, Mountains, Enemy Territory).

3. Traversing

Moving Along a Path: The table is moved from station to station (A to B to C). At each station, a backsight is taken to orient the table, and a foresight is taken to the next station.
✓ Best for Route Surveys (Roads, Rivers) and connecting stations.

4. Resection

Finding Your Own Location: Used when the instrument station is unknown. The surveyor sights 2 or 3 known, already plotted points (A, B, C). Drawing rays backwards from these points intersects at your exact location (p) on the map.
✓ Best for Locating the Instrument Station (Three-Point Problem).
