Fundamental Relationships

The axis of the plate level must lie in a plane perpendicular to the vertical axis. The vertical axis will be truly vertical when the bubble is in the centre of its run.
The line of collimation must be perpendicular to the horizontal axis at its intersection with the vertical axis.
The horizontal axis must be perpendicular to the vertical axis.
The axis of the altitude level (or telescope level) must be parallel to line of collimation.
The vertical circle vernier must read zero when the line of collimation is horizontal.
