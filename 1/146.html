Technical Notes

Select a node in the diagram to view detailed surveying principles and formulas.
Closed Traverse: A series of connected lines whose end point coincides with the starting point. Essential for mapping and large-scale engineering.
Angular Check: Sum of interior angles must equal (2n - 4) x 90°, where 'n' is the number of sides.
Linear Check: The algebraic sum of Latitudes (ΣL) and Departures (ΣD) must both equal zero.
Bowditch Rule: Also known as the Compass Rule. Used when linear and angular measurements are equally precise. Correction is proportional to the length of the side.
Transit Rule: Used when angular measurements are more precise than linear measurements (e.g., using a high-precision theodolite).
Graphical Method: Used for rough traverses where corrections are applied visually to the plotted points, often using a "correction line" construction.
Adjustment: If the closing error exceeds the permissible limit, the work must be re-run in the field. If within limits, mathematical balancing (Bowditch/Transit) is applied.
