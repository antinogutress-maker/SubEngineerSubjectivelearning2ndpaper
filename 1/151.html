1. Contour Spacing

The horizontal distance between contours indicates the slope.
• Closely Spaced: Steep Slope
• Widely Spaced: Gentle / Flat Slope
• Equally Spaced: Uniform Slope

2. Hill vs. Depression

Closed contours represent peaks or holes.
• Hill/Ridge: Values increase towards the center.
• Depression: Values decrease towards the center (often marked with hachures).

3. V-Shape & U-Shape

• V-Shaped (Valley): The 'V' points upstream towards higher elevations. Streams run through them.
• U-Shaped (Ridge): The 'U' points downhill towards lower elevations. Water sheds off them.

4. Perpendicular Intersections

90° Crossing: Contour lines must always cross ridge lines and valley lines (streams) at exactly right angles (90 degrees). They form curves of maximum slope.

5. Continuous Loops

Contours Must Close: A contour line cannot just end abruptly in the middle of a map. It must close upon itself, either within the limits of the map or outside the boundaries of the map.
