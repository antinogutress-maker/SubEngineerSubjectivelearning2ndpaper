House Layout Process

Top-View Site Simulation

AUTO-LOOP

1. Full Outline (3-4-5 Method)

Goal: Establish a perfectly square house footprint on the ground.

  • The Baseline: Fix the front building line by driving pegs at corners A and B.
  • Squaring Corner A: Measure 3m along the baseline. Strike a 4m arc upward from A, and a 5m arc diagonally from the 3m mark. The intersection creates a perfect 90° angle for the side wall to peg D.
  • Squaring Corner B: Repeat the 3-4-5 process at corner B to establish a perfectly perpendicular right-side wall leading to peg C.
  • Closing & Checking: Connect D to C to finish the rectangle. Finally, measure both diagonals (A-C and B-D). If the diagonals are exactly equal, the house layout is a perfect rectangle.

2. Centre Line Marking

Goal: Transfer the plan to the ground for excavation without losing corner references.

  • Profile Boards (Hurdles): Erect wooden profile boards 1.5m to 2m outside the actual building footprint. This ensures the reference points aren't destroyed when digging begins.
  • Stretching Strings: Drive nails into the profile boards and stretch nylon strings tightly across the site. These strings represent the exact centerlines of all external and internal walls.
  • Plumb Bob Drop: Hang a plumb bob from the intersections of the strings to transfer the exact center points down to the uneven ground surface.
  • Lime Marking: Using the center points, measure half the trench width on either side. Mark the thick excavation trench outlines on the ground using highly visible white lime powder.
