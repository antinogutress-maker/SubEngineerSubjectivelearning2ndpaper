Theory of Extra Widening

When a vehicle negotiates a horizontal curve, the rear wheels do not follow the exact path of the front wheels. This phenomenon is called off-tracking. To provide safe passage, extra width ($W_e$) is provided on curves. It consists of two parts:

  1. Mechanical Widening ($W_m$): To account for the physical off-tracking of the vehicle due to its rigid wheelbase ($l$). Calculated purely by geometry.
  2. Psychological Widening ($W_{ps}$): To account for the psychological tendency of drivers to maintain a greater clearance between vehicles on curves. It is an empirical formula dependent on speed ($V$) and radius ($R$).
