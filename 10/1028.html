Stopping Sight Distance (SSD)

SSD is the minimum clear sight distance required for a driver to safely stop a vehicle traveling at design speed.

Overtaking Sight Distance (OSD)

OSD is the minimum distance required for a vehicle (A) to safely overtake a slow-moving vehicle (B) against opposing traffic (C).
