Sub-Engineer Duties

Deep Visual Memorization

LIVE SYNC

1. Organizing Works

Distributing tasks: Allocating manpower, assigning machinery, and routing clear instructions to site supervisors and laborers efficiently.

2. Ensuring Safety

Zero harm policy: Strictly enforcing PPE usage (Helmets, Vests), setting up hazard barriers, and actively supervising risky operations.

3. Providing Training

Toolbox Talks: Conducting skill development sessions, teaching correct construction methodologies, and raising hazard awareness.

4. Quality Control

Material & Workmanship: Continuous field tests (Slump, Compaction), checking structural alignments, and approving work against specs.

5. Record Keeping

Documentation: Maintaining daily progress reports (DPR), Measurement Books (MB), material consumption logs, and site diaries.
