I. Client (Owner)

INVEST
  • Ultimate Decision Maker.
  • Finances and offers the project.
  • Can be an individual or organization.

II. Consultant (Engineer)

BRIDGE
  • Client's representative at the site.
  • Provides drawings, specs, and gives advice.
  • Supervises work quality continuously.

III. Contractor (Builder)

EXECUTE
  • Responsible for execution of work at site.
  • Ensures timely completion of the project.
  • Handles the Defect Liability period.

Two Contracts, One Goal

The Client holds a Construction Contract with the Contractor and a Consultancy Agreement with the Engineer. The Engineer exercises Supervision, Instruction & Approval directly over the Contractor.
