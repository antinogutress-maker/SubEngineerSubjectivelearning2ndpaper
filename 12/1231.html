Types of Contracts

1. Lump Sum Contract

Fixed Price for Whole Work: The contractor agrees to execute a complete piece of work for a fixed, agreed-upon sum of money. Changes in material costs usually do not affect the final payout.

2. Item Rate Contract

Payment by Quantity: Also known as a Unit Price contract. The contractor is paid based on the actual quantities of work done (e.g., per cubic meter of concrete) at agreed-upon unit rates.

3. Labour Contract

Owner Provides Materials: The contractor provides only the labor, tools, and scaffolding required. The client/owner supplies all the construction materials (bricks, cement, steel).

4. Cost Plus Contract

Actual Cost + Profit Fee: The contractor is reimbursed for the actual costs incurred during construction, plus a fixed percentage or lump sum as their guaranteed profit fee.

5. B.O.O.T. Contract

Build-Own-Operate-Transfer: A private entity Finances & Builds the project, Owns & Operates it for a concession period to recover costs (e.g., collecting tolls), and then Transfers ownership to the government.
