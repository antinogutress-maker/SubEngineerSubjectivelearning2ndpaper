Pre-Tender Preparation

Deep Visual Memorization

AUTO-LOOP

1. Project Formulation

Feasibility & Necessity: The very first step. A thorough study is conducted to check if the project is technically feasible, economically viable, and actually necessary for the public/client.

2. Design & Quantity

Blueprints & BOQ: Engineers create detailed working drawings. From these drawings, the exact quantities of materials (cement, steel, bricks) and labor required are calculated.

3. Cost Estimation

Applying Rates: The calculated quantities are multiplied by the approved schedule of rates (District Rates) to determine the total anticipated cost of the project.

4. Approval of Estimate

Technical Sanction: The detailed cost estimate and designs are submitted to the competent authority for review and final approval (T/S) before any financial commitments are made.

5. Budget Resource Planning

Phasing the Funds: Arranging and securing the budget for different stages of construction (e.g., 20% for Foundation, 40% for Superstructure) so cash flow doesn't halt progress.

6. Tender Doc Preparation

The Rulebook: Compiling the Notice Inviting Tender (NIT), Instructions to Bidders, BOQ, General Conditions (GCC), Special Conditions (SCC), and Drawings into one comprehensive legal package.

7. Invitation of Bids

Going Public: Publishing the Tender Notice in national dailies or e-procurement portals. Calling capable contractors to submit their sealed competitive bids.
