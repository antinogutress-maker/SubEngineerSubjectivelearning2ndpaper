1. The Contract Structure

The Two Pillars: Construction contracts contain two main rulebooks. The thick, pre-printed General Conditions, and the tailored, project-specific Special Conditions that are attached on top.

2. General Conditions (GCC)

The Standard Boilerplate: Standard rules that apply to all projects. They define universal rights, obligations, payment terms, dispute resolution, and standard time extensions. They rarely change.

3. Special Conditions (SCC)

The Custom Modifications: Project-specific additions, deletions, or amendments to the GCC. This includes specific site working hours, local environmental laws, or unique material requirements.

4. Order of Precedence

SCC Wins The Clash: The Golden Rule of contracts. If there is ever a contradiction or conflict between the two, the Special Conditions (SCC) always overrule the General Conditions (GCC).
