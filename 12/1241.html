Approvals & Sanctions

Construction Management

AUTO-LOOP

1. Administrative Approval (A/A)

The "Idea & Budget" Green Light: Formal acceptance of the proposal by the competent authority. It implies that the project is necessary and that the department is willing to fund it. Based on preliminary estimates.

2. Technical Sanction (T/S)

The "Engineering" Guarantee: Detailed estimate, design, calculations, and specifications are checked by a competent engineering authority. It guarantees the project is structurally sound and accurately estimated.

3. The Strict Sequence

Chronological Order: Technical Sanction (T/S) can never be given unless Administrative Approval (A/A) has already been obtained. You cannot design the details if the budget/idea isn't approved first.

4. Expenditure Sanction

Releasing the Funds: Even after A/A and T/S, money cannot be spent. Expenditure Sanction is the final act of actually allotting the specific funds to the project so physical work can commence.
