Procurement Methods

Construction Management

AUTO-LOOP

1. Direct Purchase

LIMIT: ≤ Rs. 5 Lakhs

Fast & Simple: Buying materials or services directly from a single supplier without competition. Used for low-value, urgent, or proprietary items.

2. Sealed Quotation

LIMIT: Rs. 5 to 20 Lakhs

Minimum 3 Bidders: Asking for rates in sealed envelopes from at least three suppliers. Faster than open bidding, used for medium-low value works.

3. Users Committee

LIMIT: ≤ Rs. 1 Crore (10M)

Community Involvement: Local beneficiaries execute the work themselves. Promotes local employment. Heavy machinery is strictly prohibited.

4. NBC (National Bidding)

LIMIT: Rs. 20 Lakhs to 100 Crore

National Competitive Bidding (NCB): Open tender published in a National Daily Newspaper. Only contractors registered within the country can participate.

5. IBC (International Bidding)

LIMIT: > Rs. 100 Crore (1 Billion)

International Competitive Bidding (ICB): For mega projects requiring advanced tech or massive capital. Advertised globally in English. Foreign bidders allowed.
