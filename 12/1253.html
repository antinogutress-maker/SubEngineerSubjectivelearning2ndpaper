Construction Lifecycle

Deep Visual Memorization

AUTO-LOOP

1. Initiation Phase

The Idea & Charter: The project is born. Feasibility studies are conducted, the business case is made, and the Project Charter is signed. The Project Manager is officially appointed.

2. Planning Phase

Blueprints & Schedules: The "How-To" guide is created. This includes detailed engineering blueprints, budgeting, resource allocation, risk management plans, and Gantt/CPM schedules.

3. Execution Phase

Putting Plans to Action: The actual physical construction takes place. Labor, materials, and machinery are deployed. This phase consumes the most budget and time.

4. Controlling & Monitoring

Keeping on Track: Runs parallel to Execution. Measuring actual progress against the planned baseline. Includes quality checks, cost tracking, and taking corrective actions if delayed.

5. Closing Phase

Handover: The project is officially finished. Conducting final inspections, handing over the keys/facility to the owner, releasing staff/equipment, and archiving project records.
