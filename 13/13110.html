Crushing Test of Bricks

Measuring the Compressive Strength of Masonry Bricks

🧱 Preparation & Procedure

  • Immersion: The brick is immersed in water for 24 hours at room temperature.
  • Frog Filling: The "frog" (the depression on top of the brick) is filled flush with 1:3 cement mortar and cured.
  • Plywood Sheets: The brick is placed between two 3mm thick plywood sheets in the Compressive Testing Machine (CTM) to ensure the load is distributed evenly.
  • Applying Load: The load is applied uniformly at a rate of 14 N/mm²/minute until the brick fails (shatters).

📐 Calculation & Grading

Compressive Strength = Max Load at Failure (N) / Area (mm²)

For a standard brick (190mm x 90mm), the Area = 17,100 mm².

Standard Grades (Nepal/India):
First Class Brick: Minimum 10.5 N/mm² (MPa)
Second Class Brick: Minimum 7.0 N/mm² (MPa)
Third Class Brick: Minimum 3.5 N/mm² (MPa)
