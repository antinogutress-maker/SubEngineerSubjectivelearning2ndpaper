Aggregate Impact Value (AIV)

Measuring the Toughness of Aggregates against Sudden Shocks

⚙️ Apparatus & Preparation

  • Machine: Steel frame with a hammer weighing exactly 13.5 to 14 kg.
  • The Drop: Hammer drops from a strict height of 380 mm (± 5 mm).
  • IS Sieves: 12.5 mm, 10 mm, and 2.36 mm.
  • Sample Prep: Aggregates passing 12.5 mm but retained on 10 mm are oven-dried.
  • Filling: The cylindrical cup is filled in 3 layers, each tamped 25 times. The total weight is W₁.

💥 Procedure & Calculation

  1. Place the cup in the machine. Raise the hammer to 380 mm and let it drop freely.
  2. Deliver exactly 15 blows.
  3. Remove the crushed sample and sieve it through the 2.36 mm IS Sieve.
  4. Weigh the dust that passes through the sieve. This is W₂.

AIV = (W₂ / W₁) × 100 %
