Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) Test

Internal Quality Assessment of Concrete (NDT)

❓ Why is it done?

The Rebound Hammer only tests the surface of the concrete. But what if the inside is full of holes, cracks, or honeycombing? We need a way to check the internal health of the structure.

The UPV test acts like a medical ultrasound for buildings. It helps engineers detect internal flaws, voids, and measure the overall uniformity and quality of the concrete without destroying it.

⚙️ How does it work?

  • Transducers: Two probes are placed on opposite sides of the concrete: a Transmitter (Tx) and a Receiver (Rx).
  • The Pulse: The Tx sends a high-frequency sound wave through the concrete. The machine measures the exact Time (T) it takes to reach the Rx.
  • The Math: Velocity (V) = Distance (L) / Time (T).
  • The Logic: Sound travels fast in dense, solid concrete (> 4.5 km/s). If there are cracks or air voids, the sound wave has to travel around them, taking more time, resulting in a low velocity (< 3.0 km/s).
