Standard Consistency of Cement

Finding the Baseline Water Content (P) using Vicat Apparatus

⚙️ Apparatus & Procedure

  1. Take exactly 400g of dry cement.
  2. Add a measured percentage of water and mix thoroughly for exactly 3 to 5 minutes.
  3. Fill the brass Vicat mold with the cement paste, resting it on a non-porous glass plate, and smooth the top.
  4. Attach the standard plunger (10mm diameter, 50mm length). Lower it to the paste surface.
  5. Release it quickly, allowing it to sink under its own weight (300g total moving mass).

🎯 The Target Result

If the paste is too dry, the plunger won't sink deeply. If it's too wet, it will sink all the way to the bottom plate (40 mm).

Standard Consistency (P) is achieved ONLY when the plunger penetrates exactly 33 mm to 35 mm from the top (which is 5 mm to 7 mm from the bottom).

This "P" value is crucial because it acts as the baseline water content for subsequent tests like Initial/Final Setting Time and Soundness tests.
