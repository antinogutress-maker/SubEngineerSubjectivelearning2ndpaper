Compressive Strength of Cement

Testing Standard 70.6 mm Cement Mortar Cubes

🧱 Preparation & Curing

  • Mix Ratio: 1:3 (Cement : Standard Sand). Dry mix for 1 min, add exact water, then gauge for 3 to 4 minutes.
  • Molding: Place in a 70.6 mm cube mold. Compact using a vibration machine (12000 ± 400 vpm) for exactly 2 minutes.
  • Curing: Keep in a damp room (≥ 90% humidity) for 24 hours. Submerge demolded cubes in clean water until the exact testing day.

📏 Testing & Calculation

  • Surface Area: The 70.6 mm cube has an exact bearing area of 5000 mm².
  • Loading: The load is applied smoothly at a rate of 35 N/mm²/minute.
  • Result: Test 3 cubes for a specific age and take the average. Reject any cube that varies by more than ± 10% from the average.
Strength (MPa) = Failure Load (N) / 5000 mm²
