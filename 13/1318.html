Penetration Test of Bitumen

Measuring Bitumen Hardness & Grading

🔬 Apparatus & Procedure

  • Penetrometer: Holds the needle and dial gauge.
  • Standard Needle: The needle, moving shaft, and standard weight total exactly 100 grams.
  • Water Bath: Sample is kept exactly at 25°C.
  • Procedure: The needle tip touches the surface (Dial=0). The 100g needle is released for exactly 5 seconds.

🇳🇵 Conclusion & Grading (Real-World)

The penetration depth is measured in tenths of a millimeter (0.1 mm).

If the needle penetrates 6.5 mm (65 units), it ranges in 60-70. We call this 60/70 grade bitumen.

Practical Knowledge:
A higher number (80/100) means it's softer—used in cold climates like the Himalayas.
A lower number (30/40) means it's harder—used in hot climates like the Terai to prevent rutting.
Sample conditioned at 25°C. Needle set to zero.
