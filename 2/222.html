1. Raw Extraction

Limestone and Clay are fed continuously. Smooth counter-rotating crushers break them down before fine grinding in tube mills.

2. Blending Phase

Ground materials are stored in silos and precisely blended into a chemically optimal Raw Mix.

3. Thermal Synthesis

The Raw Mix enters the rotating kiln. Intense heat (up to 1450°C) triggers chemical fusion, forming glowing clinker nodules.

4. Cooling & Milling

Red-hot clinkers are rapidly cooled. They undergo fine grinding alongside 3-5% Gypsum to regulate setting times.

5. Dispatch

The final automated flow routes the finished Portland Cement to massive dispatch silos for immediate distribution.
