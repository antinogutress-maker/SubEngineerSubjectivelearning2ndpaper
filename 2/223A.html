Lab Tests of Cement

1. Fineness Test (Sieve)

Objective: Check proper grinding. Finer cement hydrates faster, yielding higher early strength.

  • Take 100g of cement and place it on an IS Sieve No. 9 (90 µm).
  • Break lumps, shake continuously for 15 minutes.
  • Weigh the residue left on the sieve.
  • Limit: Residue must not exceed 10% by weight for Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC).

2. Setting Time (Vicat App.)

Objective: Determine how fast the paste loses plasticity (Initial) and hardens (Final).

  • Mix cement with 0.85 P water (P = standard consistency).
  • Initial Set: 1mm square needle is dropped. Time when it fails to pierce block by 5-7mm from bottom. (Limit: ≥ 30 mins).
  • Final Set: 5mm annular collar attachment fails to make an impression on the surface. (Limit: ≤ 10 hours).

3. Soundness (Le Chatelier)

Objective: Detect presence of uncombined Lime/Magnesia which causes dangerous delayed expansion.

  • Fill Le Chatelier split brass cylinder with paste.
  • Submerge in water, bring to boil, boil for 3 hours.
  • Measure the distance between the indicator arms.
  • Limit: The expansion of arms must not exceed 10mm.

4. Compressive Strength

Objective: Determine the structural load-bearing capacity of the cement mortar.

  • Mix 1 part cement with 3 parts standard Ennore sand.
  • Cast in 70.6 mm face area cubes (50 sq.cm area).
  • Cure in water. Test in Compressive Testing Machine (CTM) after 3, 7, and 28 days.
  • Limit (OPC 33): 3-day strength ≥ 16 N/mm², 28-day ≥ 33 N/mm².
