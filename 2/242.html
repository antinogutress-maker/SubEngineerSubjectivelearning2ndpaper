Paint Preparation Process

Manufacturing Stages

AUTO-LOOP

1. Grinding the Base

The raw base material (like White Lead) is fed into a grinding mill. A small quantity of oil is added, and it is heavily ground until it forms a smooth, thick, lump-free paste.

2. Mixing the Vehicle

The thick base paste is transferred to a mixing vat. The main vehicle (e.g., Linseed Oil) is gradually poured in while stirring continuously to create a uniform liquid suspension.

3. Adding Pigment

Fine coloring pigments are introduced into the mixture. It is thoroughly stirred mechanically so the color disperses perfectly throughout the entire batch of paint.

4. Thinner & Drier

Solvent (Thinner) is added to reduce the viscosity to a workable consistency. Finally, a Drier is mixed in to accelerate the oxygen absorption and hardening process of the final paint.

5. Straining (Filtering)

The final mixed paint is poured through a fine mesh, cloth, or sieve. This crucial last step filters out any unground lumps, impurities, or coagulated particles, yielding pure, smooth paint.
