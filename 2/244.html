Ingredients of Paint

Construction Materials

AUTO-LOOP

1. The Base (Body)

Provides Bulk & Opacity: The principal solid constituent (powder). It forms the bulk of the paint, hides the surface underneath, and prevents shrinkage cracks.
Examples: White Lead, Red Lead, Zinc Oxide, Iron Oxide.

2. Vehicle (Binder)

Holds it Together: The liquid substance that holds the base and pigment in suspension. It allows the paint to be spread evenly and dries to form a tough binder film.
Examples: Linseed Oil, Tung Oil, Nut Oil.

3. Pigments

Imparts Color: Fine solid particles added to the paint specifically to give it the desired aesthetic color and to protect the paint film by reflecting destructive UV rays.
Examples: Lamp Black (Black), Umber (Brown), Chrome Yellow.

4. Solvent (Thinner)

Makes it Workable: A volatile liquid added to thin the paint, reducing its viscosity so it can be easily brushed or sprayed. It evaporates entirely after application.
Examples: Turpentine, Mineral Spirits, Naphtha.

5. Drier

Accelerates Drying: Chemical compounds that absorb oxygen from the air and transfer it to the vehicle (oil), drastically speeding up the hardening and drying process of the paint film.
Examples: Litharge, Red Lead, Zinc Sulphate.
