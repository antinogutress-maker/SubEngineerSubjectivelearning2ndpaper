Application of Paints

Standard Exam Sequence

AUTO-LOOP

1. Surface Preparation

Cleaning & Sanding: The foundation of good painting. The surface must be thoroughly cleaned of dirt, grease, and old loose paint. It is then rubbed down with pumice stone or sandpaper to create a smooth, clean base.

2. Knotting & Stopping

Sealing & Filling: 'Knotting' seals wood knots to prevent resin from ruining the paint. 'Stopping' (Puttying) uses putty to fill nail holes, cracks, and dents, leveled off with a putty knife.

3. Priming (Primer Coat)

The Adhesion Layer: The very first coat of paint. It penetrates the surface pores, seals them to prevent absorption of the actual paint, and provides a strong grip (adhesion) for subsequent coats.

4. Undercoating

Building Opacity: Applied over the primer. Its main job is to build up the thickness, completely hide the surface color/grain beneath, and provide a uniform, flat base for the final finish.

5. Finishing Coat

Protection & Gloss: The final topcoat. It is applied carefully to leave no brush marks. It provides the final desired color, aesthetic gloss, and robust protection against weather and wear.
