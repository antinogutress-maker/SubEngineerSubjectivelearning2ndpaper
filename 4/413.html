Pascal's Law & Its Proof

Loksewa Exam: 5 Marks
"The intensity of pressure at any point in a fluid at rest is the same in all directions."

Step 1: Setup

Consider a tiny wedge-shaped fluid element ABC at rest. Since it's at rest, the sum of horizontal and vertical forces must be zero.

Pressure acts normally to each face.

Step 2: Resolve Pz

The inclined pressure Pz acts on face AB. We resolve it into components:

  • Horizontal: Pz sin θ
  • Vertical: Pz cos θ

Step 3: Horizontal Balance

ΣFx = 0

Px (AC × 1) = Pz (AB × 1) sin θ

Since sin θ = AC/AB:
Px (AC) = Pz (AB) (AC/AB)
Px = Pz

Step 4: Vertical Balance

ΣFy = 0

Py (BC × 1) = Pz (AB × 1) cos θ

Since cos θ = BC/AB:
Py (BC) = Pz (AB) (BC/AB)
Py = Pz

*Note: Weight of the tiny element is negligible (dW ≈ 0).

Conclusion

From our calculations:
Px = Py = Pz

This proves that the intensity of pressure at any point in a fluid at rest is the same in all directions.
