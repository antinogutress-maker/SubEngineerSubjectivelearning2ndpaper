Terzaghi's Principle of Effective Stress

Loksewa Format: 5 Marks

1. Principle / Definition (1 Mark):

Terzaghi's principle states that the total stress (σ) at any point within a saturated soil mass is made up of two distinct parts: the effective stress (σ') carried by the soil skeleton through particle-to-particle contact, and the pore water pressure (u) carried by the water in the continuous void spaces.

2. Mathematical Equation (1 Mark):

σ = σ' + u   &implies;   σ' = σ - u

3. Physical Significance of Effective Stress (1.5 Marks):

  • Effective stress (σ') is not a physical force that can be measured directly; it is an abstract calculated quantity.
  • It represents the true stress that dictates the physical behavior of the soil.
  • Water cannot resist shear stress. Therefore, pore water pressure (u) acts hydrostatically and equally in all directions, having no effect on the arrangement of soil particles.

4. Engineering Applications (1.5 Marks):

  • Shear Strength: The shear strength of soil (τ) is directly proportional to the effective stress, not total stress (τ = c' + σ' \tan φ'). A decrease in effective stress (e.g., due to rising water table) reduces soil stability.
  • Consolidation: All measurable compression (settlement) in soils is caused exclusively by changes in effective stress.
