Calculations

1. Total Load (incl. 10% self-wt)
Wt = W + 0.1W
Wt = 50 + 5 = 55.0 kN/m
2. Required Base Width
Breq = Wt / SBC
Breq = 55.0 / 100 = 0.550 m (550 mm)
3. PCC Bed Geometry
Offsetm = 50 mm
Thickpcc = Offsetm + 50 = 100 mm
4. Steps & Final Dimensions
Steps (N) required to reach Breq:
N = 2
Bprov = t + 2(N × Offsetm) + 2(Thickpcc)
Bprov = 630 mm
Df = Thickpcc + (N × Heightstep)
Df = 300 mm
