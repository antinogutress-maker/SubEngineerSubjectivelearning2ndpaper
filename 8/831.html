Step-by-Step Calculation

1. Sanitary Sewage (DWF)
Assuming 80% reaches sewer & Peak Factor = 2
DWF = (Pop × Rate × 0.8) / (1000 × 24 × 3600)
DWF = 0.25 m³/s
Max Sanitary = 2 × DWF = 0.50 m³/s
2. Storm Water (Rational Method)
Tc = Te + Tf = 25 min
Since Tc > 20, I = 1020 / (t + 20)
I = 22.67 mm/hr
Qstorm = (C × I × A) / 360
Qstorm = (0.45 × 22.67 × 150) / 360
Qstorm = 4.25 m³/s
3. Sewer Sizing & Velocity
Combined Q = Qsan + Qstorm = 4.75 m³/s
Area Req = Q / Vmax = 1.48 m²
Dia Req = √(4A / π) = 1.374 m
Adopted D = 1.4 m
Areaact = (π/4) × D² = 1.54 m²
Vact = Q / Areaact = 3.08 m/s
3.08 < 3.2 m/s (SAFE)
